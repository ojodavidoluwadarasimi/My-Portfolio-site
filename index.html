Nov 5, 2026 AI Tools

Using AI as a thinking partner, not a code generator

I've stopped treating AI as a magic code writer. Instead I use it to challenge my assumptions, generate test cases I didn't think of, and explain concepts from different angles. The real skill is knowing what questions to ask and how to verify the answers.

Oct 28, 2026 Debugging

The CBT palette bug that taught me about state

While building ScoreWise I had a subtle bug where flagged questions would sometimes lose their status after switching subjects. Tracking it down forced me to think carefully about where state lives and when it gets overwritten. Simple apps still teach deep lessons.

Oct 15, 2026 Systems

Why I'm studying systems and quant thinking early

Software engineering is more than shipping features. Understanding how systems scale, how data moves, and how uncertainty is handled gives me a better foundation for AI work and for building things that last. I'm starting with fundamentals rather than jumping straight into frameworks.
